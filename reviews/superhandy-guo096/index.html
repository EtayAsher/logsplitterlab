Power and expected workload

Fits higher-volume gas splitting where the ability to split large rounds vertically, without lifting them onto a beam, matters more than the fastest possible cycle time.

Cycle time

A 12-second cycle time, per the manufacturer, is slower than the Landworks GUO079 in our data (7.5 seconds) — but this model's larger 25-ton rating and vertical operation may matter more for some buyers.

Log size considerations

Rated for logs up to 24 in. diameter and 20 in. length, per the manufacturer — the largest diameter capacity in our current data.

Portability

Moves via 10 in. wheels and a folding handle rather than a confirmed vehicle tow hitch — treat it as property-portable, not road-towable, unless you confirm otherwise with the manufacturer. Unit weight is not published here because sources conflict — see source notes.

Setup and storage

Hydraulic fluid is not included and must be added before first use. For storage, retract the ram and stabilize or drain fuel per standard small-engine practice if storing over winter.

Noise and fumes

Gas-powered, so expect engine noise and exhaust; no electrical hookup required.

Usability

Half-beam design converts between horizontal and vertical operation, per the manufacturer — useful for large rounds you don't want to lift onto a beam.

Maintenance expectations

Follow the 7HP engine manufacturer's oil and service schedule, and check hydraulic fluid level before each use. Fluid types and service intervals vary by model — always follow the manufacturer's manual rather than a generic schedule. See our maintenance guide for general upkeep concepts.

Safety notes

Operate this splitter only as described in the manufacturer's manual. Keep hands clear of the wedge and ram path, split one log at a time unless the machine is explicitly rated otherwise, and wear eye protection and sturdy footwear. See our general safety guidance — it does not replace the manufacturer's instructions for this specific model.

Who should buy it

Choose this if you need more tonnage or vertical operation for large rounds, and don't need the fastest cycle time or a confirmed tow rating.

Who should choose another product

If cycle time matters most to you and you don't need vertical operation, the Landworks GUO079 splits nearly twice as fast at a lower (but still substantial) 20-ton rating. See our Best Gas Log Splitters roundup for other verified options.

Compared with a realistic alternative

Compared to the Landworks GUO079, this model offers more tonnage (25T vs. 20T) and horizontal/vertical operation, but at a slower cycle time (12s vs. 7.5s) — worth weighing against how much you split and whether vertical operation matters to you. Read the Landworks GUO079 summary.
