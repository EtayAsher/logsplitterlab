Usability

The four-way Cross Wedge is the defining feature: rather than pushing a log through a single blade to split it in two, it pushes the log through four blades arranged in a cross, dividing it into quarters in one stroke. This can mean fewer total strokes to reach kindling-sized pieces, but it also means the wedge is less forgiving of an off-center or irregular round than a standard two-piece wedge.

FAQ

What does the "Cross Wedge" actually do?

It splits one log into four pieces in a single stroke, using four blades arranged in a cross instead of the single blade most splitters use to split a log in two. It works best on reasonably round, centered logs.

Why doesn't this review list a maximum log diameter or length?

We found a tonnage-labeling conflict between this exact listing (rated 7 tons on its own product page) and a closely related PROYAMA model marketed at 6.5 tons under a different ASIN.
