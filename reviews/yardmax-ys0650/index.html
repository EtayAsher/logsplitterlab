A 6.5-ton electric splitter for light, frequent use near a garage or shed outlet — currently the only electric model in our catalog.

Key strengths

  • No fuel, exhaust, or engine maintenance — plug in and go
  • 20.5 in. log capacity on a 15A motor
  • Ships with a removable stand and log trays to catch split pieces, per the manufacturer

Key limitations

  • 6.5-ton rating is on the low end for splitting dense or large-diameter hardwood
  • Tied to a power outlet and appropriately rated extension cord
  • 19-second cycle time is longer than any gas model in our catalog

Power and expected workload

Best suited to light, frequent splitting sessions rather than processing a large volume in one sitting.

Cycle time

A 19-second cycle time, per manufacturer specifications, is on the slower end compared to the gas models in our catalog (7.5–15.3 seconds) — worth factoring in if you're processing a large batch in one session.

Log size considerations

Rated for logs up to 10 in. diameter and 20.5 in. length, per the manufacturer.

Portability

Not designed for towing; repositioning relies on its own 7 in. wheels for moving it around a garage or yard.

Setup and storage

Ships with a removable stand and log trays, per the manufacturer — use with or without the stand based on preference. Store indoors or under cover; there's no fuel to manage before storage, but check hydraulic fluid periodically.

Noise and electrical requirements

Runs on a standard 120V/15A circuit — confirm your outlet's circuit isn't already loaded with other tools, and use an appropriately rated extension cord if needed. Quieter than a gas engine, with no exhaust.

Usability

Motor draws up to 15A on a standard 120V circuit, per the manufacturer; confirm your circuit can handle that draw before running it continuously.

Maintenance expectations

Check the hydraulic fluid level and inspect the power cord and plug before each use, per the manufacturer's manual. Fluid types and service intervals vary by model — always follow the manufacturer's manual rather than a generic schedule. See our maintenance guide for general upkeep concepts.

Safety notes

Operate this splitter only as described in the manufacturer's manual. Keep hands clear of the wedge and ram path, split one log at a time unless the machine is explicitly rated otherwise, and wear eye protection and sturdy footwear. See our general safety guidance — it does not replace the manufacturer's instructions for this specific model.

Who should buy it

FAQ

Does the YS0650 come with a stand?

Yes — per the manufacturer, it ships with a removable stand and log trays that catch split pieces as they fall.

Will the YS0650 split oak or other hardwood?

It can split smaller-diameter hardwood within its 10 in. diameter rating, but at 6.5 tons it will struggle more with large, dense, or knotty rounds than a higher-tonnage gas splitter would.
