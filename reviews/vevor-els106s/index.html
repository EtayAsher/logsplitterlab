Verdict

A 6.5-ton electric splitter whose published specifications are strikingly close to the BILT HARD TLA-0101 already in our catalog — same log capacity, same motor speed, nearly identical weight and dimensions. Unlike our disclosed Landworks/SuperHandy gas pair, where a shared manufacturer (GCM) is named directly on both listings, we found no evidence connecting these two products to a common manufacturer — the specifications happen to match closely; we can't explain why.

Best for

Homeowners doing light, frequent splitting of softer-to-medium wood within reach of an outlet who want the extra precision of VEVOR's specific, manufacturer-published extension-cord requirement rather than generic guidance.

Key limitation

Very dense hardwood, logs longer than 20 in. or thicker than 9.8 in., or anyone specifically wanting a specialized outdoor-power-equipment brand rather than VEVOR's broad, multi-category import catalog.

Power and expected workload

Best suited to light, frequent splitting sessions rather than processing a large volume in one sitting — functionally the same fit as the other 6.5-ton electric models in our catalog.

Cycle time

Not published by the manufacturer for this model — we won't estimate one. Every other electric model in our catalog publishes a cycle time; this is a genuine gap in VEVOR's own documentation, not an oversight on our part.

Log size considerations

Rated for logs up to 9.8 in. diameter and 20 in. length, per the manufacturer — within a fraction of an inch of the BILT HARD TLA-0101's 9.8 in./20.5 in. rating.

Portability

6 in. transport wheels make it reasonably easy to reposition around a garage or yard; it is not designed for towing behind a vehicle.

Setup and storage

Assembly is required on first setup. Store indoors or under cover; there's no fuel to manage before storage, but check hydraulic fluid periodically.

Noise and electrical requirements

Runs on a standard 120V/15A circuit at 1500W, per the manufacturer, which explicitly specifies a maximum 50 ft. extension cord with at least a 3.3 mm² cross-section — worth checking against whatever cord you already own before assuming it's adequate. Quieter than a gas engine, with no exhaust.

Usability

Motor draws up to 15A on a standard 120V circuit at 1500W and up to 3,400 RPM, per the manufacturer, producing 6.5 tons of force through a standard steel wedge; confirm your circuit can handle the draw before running it continuously.

Maintenance expectations

Check the hydraulic fluid level (3.2L capacity, per the manufacturer) and inspect the power cord and plug before each use. Fluid types and service intervals vary by model — always follow the manufacturer's manual rather than a generic schedule. See our maintenance guide for general upkeep concepts.

Safety notes

Operate this splitter only as described in the manufacturer's manual. Keep hands clear of the wedge and ram path, split one log at a time unless the machine is explicitly rated otherwise, and wear eye protection and sturdy footwear. See our general safety guidance — it does not replace the manufacturer's instructions for this specific model.

FAQ

Is the VEVOR ELS106S the same machine as the BILT HARD TLA-0101 in your catalog?

We can't confirm they share a manufacturer, but their published specifications are remarkably close — same log capacity, same 3,400 RPM motor speed, and nearly identical weight and dimensions. Treat them as very closely matched rather than assuming either is meaningfully different.

What extension cord does the VEVOR ELS106S need?

The manufacturer specifies a maximum 50 ft. cord length with a minimum 3.3 mm² wire cross-section (roughly 12-gauge). Check this against your cord before assuming a generic outdoor extension cord will do.
