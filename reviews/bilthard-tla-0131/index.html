Verdict

A 27-ton gas splitter with horizontal/vertical operation and towing hardware, sitting between the two 20-ton portable models and the 32-ton YARDMAX in our catalog — a real midrange step up in force and capability, bought with an important caveat: BILT HARD's own warranty does not cover Amazon purchases.

Best for

Homeowners or rural property owners who've outgrown a 20-ton portable splitter but don't need the YARDMAX YU3266's full 32-ton, full-beam scale, and who want horizontal/vertical operation and towing hardware in between.

Key limitation

Anyone who wants manufacturer warranty coverage on their purchase (not available through Amazon for this product), or who needs a confirmed unit weight, dimensions, or road-tow speed rating before buying — none of those are published by the manufacturer for this model.

  • 27 tons of force, horizontal/vertical operation, per the manufacturer — meaningfully more capable than the horizontal-only 20-ton pair in our catalog
  • Towing hardware (2-inch ball coupler, 16-inch DOT tires) confirmed by both the manufacturer and the Amazon listing's own title, which explicitly describes it as "Towable"
  • 13-second cycle time, per the manufacturer — faster than the YARDMAX YU3266's 15.3 seconds, though slower than the 20-ton pair's 7.5 seconds

  • BILT HARD's own warranty policy explicitly excludes purchases made through Amazon or any other third-party marketplace — see the warranty note below before assuming any coverage
  • Unit weight, product dimensions, hydraulic fluid type/capacity, and a confirmed road-tow speed rating are not published by the manufacturer for this model and are not shown here — we found unattributable figures for some of these on secondary sources but could not confirm them on the manufacturer's own page, so we left them out rather than publish an unverified number
  • Slower cycle time than either 20-ton model in our catalog (13s vs. 7.5s), the tradeoff for the extra tonnage and horizontal/vertical capability

Power and expected workload

Fits a homeowner or rural property owner processing more wood, or larger/tougher rounds, than a 20-ton portable comfortably handles — without needing the YARDMAX YU3266's largest-in-catalog 32-ton scale.

Cycle time

A 13-second cycle time, per the manufacturer, lands between the two 20-ton models in our catalog (7.5 seconds each) and the YARDMAX YU3266 (15.3 seconds) — a real middle point, not just a middle tonnage.

Log size considerations

Rated for logs up to 24 in. long, per the manufacturer — longer than either 20-ton model's 20 in. rating, though shorter than the YARDMAX YU3266's 26 in. A maximum log diameter is not published by the manufacturer for this model, so we're not stating one.

Portability

Ships with a 2-inch ball coupler and 16-inch DOT-rated tires, per the manufacturer, and the Amazon listing's own title describes it as towable — real, confirmed towing hardware. Unlike the YARDMAX YU3266 in our catalog, whose manufacturer publishes a specific 45 mph tow rating, we found no manufacturer-published road-speed rating for this model; confirm towing limits with the manufacturer or a licensed transport professional before towing on public roads.

Setup and storage

Assembly requirements are not detailed on the manufacturer's product page. Store indoors or under cover; stabilize or drain fuel per standard small-engine practice if storing over winter, and retract the ram fully.

Noise and fumes

Gas-powered, so expect engine noise and exhaust; no electrical hookup required.

Usability

A 2-stage gear pump and a 23-inch cylinder stroke drive the 27-ton rating through horizontal or vertical operation, per the manufacturer. Specific pump GPM and hydraulic fluid capacity are not published for this model.

Maintenance expectations

Follow the 209cc OHV engine's oil and service schedule and check hydraulic fluid periodically — specific fluid type and capacity are not published by the manufacturer for this model, so check the included manual rather than assume a figure from another product in our catalog. Fluid types and service intervals vary by model — always follow the manufacturer's manual rather than a generic schedule. See our maintenance guide for general upkeep concepts.

Safety notes

Operate this splitter only as described in the manufacturer's manual. Keep hands clear of the wedge and ram path, split one log at a time unless the machine is explicitly rated otherwise, and wear eye protection and sturdy footwear. See our general safety guidance — it does not replace the manufacturer's instructions for this specific model.

Choose this if you need more force and horizontal/vertical operation than a 20-ton portable machine offers, want towing hardware without the YARDMAX YU3266's largest-in-catalog scale, and are comfortable buying through Amazon knowing that forfeits BILT HARD's manufacturer warranty.

FAQ

Is the BILT HARD TLA-0131 covered by a warranty if I buy it on Amazon?

No — per BILT HARD's own warranty policy, coverage "applies only to products purchased directly from the official Bilt Hard website," and explicitly states that "purchases from third-party platforms such as Amazon, Walmart, or eBay are not covered." Buying this listing through Amazon means buying without BILT HARD's manufacturer warranty.

How does the TLA-0131 compare to your 20-ton gas models?

It offers more force (27 vs. 20 tons), horizontal/vertical operation (the 20-ton pair is horizontal-only), and confirmed towing hardware — at a slower cycle time (13s vs. 7.5s) and, per BILT HARD's warranty policy, no manufacturer warranty when bought via Amazon, unlike the more standard coverage on our other gas models.

What is the maximum log diameter for the TLA-0131?

Not published by the manufacturer for this model. We confirmed a 24 in. maximum log length directly from the manufacturer's product page, but no diameter figure — rather than estimate one, we've left it unset.
