Verdict

A 37-ton gas splitter built for higher-volume rural use, with a manufacturer-confirmed towing rating and a 2-year warranty.

Best for

Rural property owners processing several cords a year who need to tow the machine between sites.

Key limitation

Anyone who wants to avoid small-engine maintenance, or needs a quiet machine to run near a house in the evening.

Key strengths

  • Manufacturer-confirmed towable to 45 mph with a 2-inch ball hitch
  • 37 tons of force from a 338cc single-cylinder engine, handling logs up to 24 in. long / 100 lb
  • 2-year limited warranty with free lifetime technical support

Key limitations

  • Gas engine requires periodic oil changes and off-season fuel stabilization
  • 16-second cycle time is slower than the Landworks GUO079's 7.5 seconds
  • Large, heavy machine — more than most occasional users need

Power and expected workload

Suited to rural property owners processing multiple cords a year who need to move the machine between sites.

Cycle time

A 16-second cycle time, per Champion's own operator manual, is slower than the Landworks GUO079 in our data (7.5 seconds) — but this model's larger 37-ton rating and confirmed towing may matter more for some buyers.

Log size considerations

Rated for logs up to 24 in. long and 100 lb, per the manufacturer's manual. Rounds longer or heavier than that should be cut down before splitting.

Portability

A tow-ready wheel setup and a 2-inch ball hitch coupler make this a confirmed towable machine, rated to 45 mph per Champion's own operator manual — confirm your tow vehicle and trailer hitch are rated accordingly before towing on public roads.

Setup and storage

Requires assembly of the tow tongue and wheel hardware per Champion's manual on first setup. For storage, retract the ram fully and, if storing over winter, stabilize or drain fuel per the engine manufacturer's guidance.

Noise and fumes

As a gas splitter, expect engine noise and exhaust — plan to operate it away from windows or close neighbors. No electrical hookup is needed.

Usability

Standard gas-splitter operation; the manufacturer's manual documents a low-oil shutoff safety feature and a 1.6-gallon fuel tank.

Maintenance expectations

Follow Champion's manual for engine oil type/interval (1.2-quart capacity, 10W-30 recommended) and hydraulic fluid level checks (6.8-gallon capacity). Inspect the tow hitch and tires before transporting. Fluid types and service intervals vary by model — always follow the manufacturer's manual rather than a generic schedule. See our maintenance guide for general upkeep concepts.

Safety notes

Operate this splitter only as described in the manufacturer's manual. Keep hands clear of the wedge and ram path, split one log at a time unless the machine is explicitly rated otherwise, and wear eye protection and sturdy footwear. See our general safety guidance — it does not replace the manufacturer's instructions for this specific model.

Who should buy it

Choose this model if you need to move a splitter between work sites on a rural property, or process enough wood that 37 tons of force and a 2-year warranty matter more than a faster cycle time.

Who should choose another product

If cycle time or a lighter, more portable unit matters more than towing capability, the Landworks GUO079 is a real alternative at a lower tonnage. See our Best Gas Log Splitters roundup for other verified options.

Compared with a realistic alternative

Compared to the Landworks GUO079, this Champion model offers nearly double the tonnage (37T vs. 20T) and a confirmed tow rating, but at a slower cycle time (16s vs. 7.5s) — worth weighing against how much you split and whether you need to tow it. Read the Landworks GUO079 summary.
